I am a **Ocean Modeler** and Postdoctoral Researcher at the [University of Washington](https://cicoes.uw.edu/) and [NOAA Pacific Marine Environmental Laboratory](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/).

I develop and evaluate high-resolution regional ocean models to understand how ocean circulation, sea ice, tropical cyclones, and marine ecosystems respond to climate variability and climate change.

My research combines numerical modeling, observations, satellite data, and machine learning to improve our understanding of regional ocean processes and their societal impacts.

### Research Interests

* Regional Ocean Modeling (MOM6)
* Physical–Biogeochemical Modeling
* Sea Ice–Ocean Interactions
* Bering Sea Climate Variability
* Indian Ocean 
* Tropical Cyclone–Ocean Interaction
* Machine Learning for Oceanography

### Current Research

**Northeast Pacific MOM6 (NEP10k-MOM6)**

Development and evaluation of a high-resolution ocean–sea ice model for the Bering Sea adn Nprtheast Pacific.

**Bay of Bengal MOM6**

Implementation of a regional MOM6 configuration to investigate monsoon-driven circulation and stratification.

**Tropical Cyclone Response**

Understanding upper-ocean cooling, mixing, nutrient injection, and biological responses to tropical cyclones.

**Ocean Biogeochemistry**

Coupled physical–biogeochemical modeling using COBALTv2.
